01 / About me

Curiosity meets impact.

I'm Duarte Dias, a Biomedical Engineer based in Portugal, interested in the ways engineering and medical research can help us better understand human physiology and improve healthcare technology.

My background includes research at RISE – Health Research Network, work related to electrocardiography and signal processing, and an Erasmus+ research internship in the Netherlands with a cardiovascular and respiratory physiology group.

Alongside scientific work, I've contributed to the Bioengineering Students' Association (NEB-FEUP/ICBAS), gaining experience in communication, digital design and teamwork.
